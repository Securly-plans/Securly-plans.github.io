EmeraldOS Gold 1S

Install the Gold_1S folder beside the other E.L.S.U.S.-compatible Gold folders. Do not rename it.

Gold 1S preserves the Gold 1R universal cloud file system and Gold 1P-based visual interface. It restores the built-in Emerald Games, adds a searchable Complete Guide pinned to the desktop and taskbar, and includes Gold Radio for compatible internet station streams.

The Complete Guide is available with F1, from the desktop, taskbar, Start, Search, First-Boot Setup, and Update Setup.

Gold Radio requires a direct HTTPS audio stream URL. A browser cannot tune physical FM or AM frequencies without external radio hardware.
