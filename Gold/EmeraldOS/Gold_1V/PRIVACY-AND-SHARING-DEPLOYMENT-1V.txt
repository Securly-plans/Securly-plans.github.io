EmeraldOS Gold 1V privacy deployment

Gold 1V implements client-side account separation, owner metadata checks, explicit file-share grants, and a per-user IndexedDB cache.

IMPORTANT: Browser JavaScript cannot provide server-enforced privacy by itself. Production privacy requires Firebase Authentication so request.auth.uid is a stable account identity, plus Firestore rules equivalent to FIRESTORE-RULES-GOLD-1V.rules. The existing custom username/password records alone do not populate request.auth. Deploy Firebase Auth or a trusted token exchange before enabling the strict rules.

Cloud VM path: emeraldOSUsers/{authenticatedUserId}/goldVM/current
Share path: emeraldGoldFileShares/{shareId}
Only selected file snapshots are placed in share documents. The rest of the VM is never copied to the share collection.
