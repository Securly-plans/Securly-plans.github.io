About Escape Road 3

Looking for a new Espace Road experience? Look no further since Escape Road 3 has arrived, offering a bunch of brand-new features for absolutely fresh gameplay. Try it now on Azgames and enjoy new driving challenges and mini-games.

What ’s New in Escape Road 3

Escape Road 3 is the next sequel to the original Escape Road game, developed by AZGames. It is a fast-paced driving and escape game where players take on the role of a getaway driver trying to evade police pursuit through chaotic city streets. The goal is still majorly about surviving the chase as long as possible while collecting in-game cash.

Escape Road 3: New Features

  • 03+ extra mythic cars, 30+ characters with different power stats to unlock
  • Expanded environments, featuring more locations to explore: City, Suburbs, Desert, Ocean, etc.
  • 8 support items, including upgrading and healing items
  • Ability to swim and control airplanes, boats, and gliders.
  • Mini-games to unlock Mythic Cars: F1 Racing, Firefighting, Zombie battles, …
  • Side mini-games: treasure hunting at sea, stopping the alien invasion of Earth, …
  • Combo system: Combine a car and an item or a car and a character to create a special car/combination.
  • Support for low-spec devices

How to Play Escape Road 3

Basically, playing this sequel is quite similar to the classic experience, where escaping the chase and avoiding crashes are primary objectives. Navigating the roads skillfully really matters. Yet, thanks to new features, you can customize for a better chance to survive.

Drive and escape

Before entering the drive, take a few moments to choose the vehicle, character, and item for a strong drive. When ready, jump into the run and control your car to avoid traffic, obstacles, and police.

Tip: Use sharp turns and smart routes to survive longer.

Take advantage of new abilities

If you end up in water, you can swim instead of instantly losing. Also, in addition to cars, you can switch vehicles like boats, planes, paragliders, etc. Adapt to the environment and master the right ability to keep escaping.

Try mini-games for rewards

Play and complete special mini-games and side activities to unlock rewards and rare cars. Of course, those challenges also help sharpen your skills for smoother performance.

Game controls

WASD: Drive

The Spacebar: Escape/enter the vehicle

F: Use the Legendary item

With a host of new features, Escape Road 3 offers a fresher driving experience that takes the classic original gameplay to the next level.
