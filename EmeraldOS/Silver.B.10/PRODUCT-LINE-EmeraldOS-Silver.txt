EmeraldOS Silver Product Line

EmeraldOS Silver is a separate EmeraldOS product line focused on a glass-like desktop experience, a more modern taskbar, Silver-branded applications, and VM-like continuity across devices.

Current release:
EmeraldOS Silver Beta 1.0

Product line direction:
- Silver should feel separate from base EmeraldOS, not just a skin.
- Silver apps should use their own names, logos, storage keys, and user experience.
- Base EmeraldOS services remain underneath for compatibility, but Silver users launch Silver-branded front ends.
- Silver should resume where the user left off after login, including across devices.

Beta 1.0 priorities:
- Silver-only desktop.
- Unique app logos.
- Silver Office Beta.
- Universal notifications.
- Resume Center and cloud VM session.
- Better first-use experience.

Future roadmap:
- Silver Beta 1.1: stronger Silver Mail interface and mail notifications.
- Silver Beta 1.2: full Silver Files rewrite.
- Silver Beta 2.0: Silver-native account shell and app install/update system.
