TESTOS 3.2.T.5 - DESKTOP FOLDERS SUITE BUILD

This build changes the desktop model:
- The desktop shows application folders instead of every application icon.
- Apps are opened from folders such as Essential Apps, Office Apps, Internet Apps, Business Apps, Creative Apps, Developer Apps, and Executive Apps.
- Locked edition folders and apps are hidden.
- Real user files and folders can still be placed directly on the desktop.
- Desktop item positions are saved locally and synced with Firebase through cloudstorage.js.

Edition order:
Economy -> Home -> Business -> Virtue -> Developer -> Executive
