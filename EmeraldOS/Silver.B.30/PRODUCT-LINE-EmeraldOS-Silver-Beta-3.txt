EmeraldOS Silver Product Line
==============================

EmeraldOS Silver Beta 3.0 is treated as a separate Silver product-line shell, not just a skin of the base EmeraldOS desktop.

Design rules:
- Vista-inspired glass UI made with original CSS.
- No Microsoft logos, Windows icons, copied wallpapers, or copied assets.
- Silver apps and hubs are user-facing. Base EmeraldOS platform services remain only as compatibility services underneath.
- Silver uses its own localStorage keys and Silver desktop renderer.

Beta 3.0 focus:
- More faithful glass/orb/sidebar visual language.
- Separate Silver desktop and Start experience.
- Consolidated hubs.
- Unique Vista-like CSS logos for apps.
- Better responsive layout.
- Application folders remain available through All Silver Apps even when removed from the desktop.
