EmeraldOS Silver Beta 2.0
Cloud Workspace, Consolidation and Vista-Logo Update

This build makes Silver feel more like its own consolidated cloud OS line.

Major changes:
- More consolidation through five primary hubs: Workspace, Drive + Office, Communications, Creator Studio, and System Center.
- Editions are temporarily removed from the Silver Beta 2.0 experience.
- Silver apps remain available through Silver Apps even if their folders are removed from the desktop.
- Desktop items are fully customizable through Silver Desktop Customizer.
- Vista-like app logos are original CSS-based glass icons, not copied Microsoft assets.
- Silver Drive + Office is more centralized and acts as the main workspace for Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, folders, recent files, starred files and cloud save/load.
- Responsive UI improvements for compact, laptop, desktop and larger screens.
- Universal Silver notifications remain available through the taskbar bell.

Important notes:
- Upload the full folder.
- OS.html loads os.js, emerald57.js and silver-beta2.js.
- Firebase files are included from the previous Silver build.
- The temporary no-editions mode applies to Silver Beta 2.0 only and can be restored later.
