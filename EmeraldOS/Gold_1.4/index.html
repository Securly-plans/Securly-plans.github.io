EmeraldOS Gold 1.4

Sign in before booting the Gold desktop. This patch restores the normal login flow, fixes boot routing, and improves Staff Edition authentication.

Normal Login

Uses your EmeraldOS / Emerald Games username and SHA-256 password hash from Firestore.

Boot Options

Press F12 during startup for Emerald Systems BIOS A1 and Emerald DOS.

Staff Edition

Press F9 or use BIOS command STAFF for staff login.

Required Login

Login is required before opening the Gold desktop. Staff tools require separate staff verification.

Enter your EmeraldOS account. After login, Gold will boot normally.
