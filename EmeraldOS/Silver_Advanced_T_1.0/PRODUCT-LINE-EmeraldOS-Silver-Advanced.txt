EmeraldOS Silver Advanced Product Line
======================================

Purpose:
EmeraldOS Silver Advanced is a separate Silver product model focused on a more advanced Aero-modern desktop experience, stronger consolidation, and a cloud workspace/virtual machine style workflow.

Design direction:
- Aero-modern glass/acrylic UI.
- Consolidated hubs.
- Responsive shell.
- Universal Action Center.
- Advanced Search and Task View.
- Restore Center for cross-device workspace continuity.

Main hubs:
- Advanced Home
- Silver Explorer
- Silver Office
- Silver Communications
- Silver Store
- Creator Studio
- Settings
- Control Panel
- Action Center
- Restore Center

Compatibility:
The build includes EmeraldOS Platform 5.7 compatibility files so existing EmeraldOS apps, Mail, Assistant, creator tools, and storage features can continue to work under the Silver Advanced shell.
