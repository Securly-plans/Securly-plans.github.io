EmeraldOS Gold Product Line
===========================

EmeraldOS Gold is an independent modern EmeraldOS product line focused on a Windows 10-inspired original desktop experience, built-in apps, setup, customization, and cloud workspace continuity.

Gold 1.0 goals:
- More complete first-boot experience.
- More complete BIOS/DOS boot identity.
- More modern Settings and personalization.
- Built-in apps styled as native Gold apps.
- Stronger restore and cross-device workspace continuation.
- Original EmeraldOS branding and assets only.
