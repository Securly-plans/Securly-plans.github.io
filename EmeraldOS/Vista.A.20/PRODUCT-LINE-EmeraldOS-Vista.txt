EmeraldOS Vista Product Line
===========================

Purpose
-------
EmeraldOS Vista is a separate EmeraldOS experience line focused on a glass-style desktop, guided setup, large control panels, sidebar gadgets, media-focused hubs, and a more approachable consumer desktop.

Current Release
---------------
EmeraldOS Vista Alpha 2.0
Codename: Aurora Glass
Base Platform: EmeraldOS 5.7

Design Rules
------------
1. Use EmeraldOS branding, names, and original interface elements.
2. Do not include Microsoft logos, Windows icons, Windows wallpapers, Windows sounds, or copied assets.
3. Keep the design Vista-inspired, not Vista-copied.
4. Maintain compatibility with EmeraldOS 5.7 cloud, mail, office, assistant, and appstore systems.
