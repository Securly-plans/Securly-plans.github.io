About Wacky Nursery

Manage a lively nursery in Wacky Nursery, a fun time-management game. Take care of babies, serve food, and keep them happy in a wild and wacky adventure.

The Ultimate Babysitting Challenge

Step into the delightful chaos of Wacky Nursery, a fast-paced time-management game. As the new nursery caretaker, your job is to keep a room full of energetic babies happy and content. You'll need to juggle multiple tasks at once—feeding them, changing their diapers, and keeping them entertained before they get fussy. The game’s colorful visuals and cheerful music make the frantic pace enjoyable. Can you handle the pressure and become the best babysitter in the land?

Controls

Mouse click: Select babies, items, and interact with objects.
