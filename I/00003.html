PROMOTION NOTICE

Subject: Promotion of Seth Flueter to Assistant Chair, Monroe Entertainment Board

This document serves as official notice of promotion issued by Monroe Entertainment Services and the Monroe Entertainment Board.

Following review of organizational contributions, leadership performance, and continued dedication to the development and advancement of Emerald Games systems and services, the Monroe Entertainment Board has approved the promotion of Seth Flueter to the position of Assistant Chair, Monroe Entertainment Board, effective immediately.

Individual Promoted

Name: Seth Flueter

Previous Position: Director, Save and Load Division

New Position: Assistant Chair, Monroe Entertainment Board

Basis for Promotion

The Monroe Entertainment Board recognizes Seth Flueter for his commendable conduct, leadership, dedication, and service to Monroe Entertainment Services.

Throughout his tenure as Director of the Save and Load Division, Seth Flueter has demonstrated a strong commitment to the continued growth and development of Emerald Games and associated services. His efforts have contributed significantly to the advancement of internal systems, user experiences, operational infrastructure, and entertainment initiatives.

The Board specifically recognizes:

  • Consistent professionalism and positive conduct.
  • Leadership within the Save and Load Division.
  • Contributions to feature development and system improvement.
  • Dedication to advancing entertainment experiences and services.
  • Commitment to organizational objectives and long-term growth.
  • Reliability in carrying out responsibilities and supporting company initiatives.
  • Continued support of innovation throughout Monroe Entertainment Services and Emerald Games operations.

Appointment

Effective immediately, Seth Flueter shall assume the position of:

Assistant Chair
Monroe Entertainment Board

As Assistant Chair, Seth Flueter shall assist in supporting the governance, development, and strategic direction of Monroe Entertainment Services and its divisions, including Emerald Games.

The position grants participation in executive discussions, board initiatives, organizational planning, and other responsibilities assigned by the Chair and Monroe Entertainment Board.

Board Statement

The Monroe Entertainment Board believes that strong leadership is essential to the continued success of the organization. This promotion reflects the Board's confidence in Seth Flueter's abilities, accomplishments, and potential to contribute further to the future development of Monroe Entertainment Services.

The Board expresses its appreciation for the work completed during his service as Director of the Save and Load Division and looks forward to his continued contributions in this expanded leadership role.

Effective Status

The following actions shall take effect immediately:

  • Recognition of Seth Flueter as Assistant Chair of the Monroe Entertainment Board.
  • Update of internal organizational records and leadership rosters.
  • Granting of privileges, responsibilities, and authorities associated with the office of Assistant Chair.
  • Inclusion in executive planning, governance discussions, and Board initiatives.

Authorization

This promotion is formally approved and authorized by:

Wyatt Monroe
Chair, Monroe Entertainment Services

With the acknowledgement and support of the:

Monroe Entertainment Board Members

Final Statement

Monroe Entertainment Services congratulates Seth Flueter on this promotion and recognizes his dedication, leadership, and contributions to the organization. The Monroe Entertainment Board extends its confidence in his continued service and leadership as Assistant Chair.
